# LAST MILE — TikTok trailer (9:16, 39.2 s, 30 fps)

A courier game set in real downtown Orlando. One order: a venti iced caramel macchiato from the Starbucks on
Orange Ave to **Kyle, Citi Tower, floor 20** (101 Lake Ave). You make it, four seconds late. Kyle takes the tip.

Timing lives in `trailer/cues.json` (150 BPM, every shot on the 0.4 s beat grid). Visuals, HUD and `audio.py` all read it.

## Citi Tower reference (from photos + street view, Feb 2026)

- NE corner of Church St & Lake Ave; Lake Ave is brick-paved; yellow curb and a stop sign at the corner.
- Ground floor glazing (dark teal glass, silver mullion grid) set back under a white overhang with downlights;
  round white columns at the overhang edge, one on the corner.
- Slatted grey metal canopy wraps the corner; dimensional brushed-metal **CITITOWER 101** letters stand on it along Lake Ave.
- Gravel beds with agaves, fan palms, boulders and a blue abstract sculpture; a tall sabal palm; spiral bike racks;
  black twin-globe lamp posts; herringbone brick panel at the north end of the glazing.
- Above: solid cream mass with a rounded corner, then rounded balconies with glass rails stacked up the corner;
  striped mosaic murals on the podium; rooftop pool deck.

## Other real-place facts used
- Church Street Station: 1889 depot beside the SunRail platform; Kia Center (ex-Amway Center) next door.
- Lake Eola fountain (Linton E. Allen Memorial, 1957): green “flying saucer”, nightly colour show; swan boats; swans.
- The VUE at Lake Eola (36 floors, 150 E Robinson), Aspire (faces the lake), Society Orlando (410 N Orange).
