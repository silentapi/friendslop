Shoot protocol (docs/DATA.md §4)
  • Same matte off-white plate, same grey board, one LED panel camera-left.
  • Unwrap everything. No wrappers, cups, sauce cups, boxes, flags, logos.
  • Drinks go in our plain clear cup. Fries are poured, not arranged.
  • 3 shots per purchase: A hero 45°, B top-down, C detail or cut in half.
  • Shoot within 10 minutes. Don't tidy the food.

Reject anything with readable text or a logo, the wrong item, a pre-recipe-change photo, blur, faces, the store interior, or a too-pretty styled look. Keys: A approve · R reject · 1–5 difficulty, all acting on the first card.
