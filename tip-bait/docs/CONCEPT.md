# TIP BAIT — concept + trailer script

## The game (mobile, 3D, portrait)
You're a delivery courier in **real downtown Orlando** (built from OpenStreetMap: real footprints, real heights, real street
names and surfaces). Every order comes with a promise. Customers dangle tips, watch you on the map, text you mid-ride,
and pull the tip the second you hand it over. You're doing your best. They are not.

- **Controls (one thumb, portrait):** swipe left/right to change lanes, swipe up to hop (curbs, rails), hold to boost the
  e-bike, tilt to keep the drink level (spill meter).
- **Core loop:** accept order → pick up → ride through real streets with live hazards → building entry (elevator / stairs) →
  hand-off → **the tip reveal** (the game's signature moment: the customer may raise, keep, or bait-and-switch).
- **Customers:** each has a mood meter and a chat thread that escalates ("where r u" → "i can see u on the map" →
  "????"). **Kyle S** (Citi Tower, floor 20) is the recurring final boss: promises $50, always takes it back. Look: 6'4" Black man, small afro, black tee, grey sweatpants, big white Jordans. In-app (order card, DMs, notifications) he is always "Kyle S".
- **Hazards, all Orlando:** LYNX buses pulling out, SunRail crossings, tourists in mouse ears, 0%→100% Florida storms on
  brick streets, elevators out of order, closed Starbucks forcing a cross-town reroute.
- **App:** in-game delivery app "Gobbl" (fictional, to avoid real-brand takedowns; one constant to swap). Real places
  (Starbucks on S Orange Ave, Citi Tower, Church Street Station, Lake Eola) appear as locations.

## Research rules applied (TikTok)
Hook in ≤2 s with a relatable POV; ~30 s; a cut every ~2 s with SFX on cuts; gameplay-first with visible mobile UI; TikTok-
native captions; text inside the safe box (x 120–900, y 250–1400 of 1080×1920); loop the last frame into the first;
comment bait ("tag your Kyle"); real-location tags.
