# TIP BAIT, v2 beat sheet (directing, not a checklist)

**Spine:** a hard-working courier fights all of Orlando to deliver on time, and the customer takes the tip anyway.

**Shape:** a fast, escalating gauntlet (cut on the beat), then the real place (Citi Tower), then everything slows to awkward held beats for the punchline. The contrast is the joke. Each beat has one job, and the cut doesn't move on until that job lands.

| # | Beat | Len | Job (what the viewer must feel) | How it lands |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook / the bait | 3.2 | "Oh no, I know this guy." | Order card on screen from frame 0. Hold to accept. Slam on the drop. |
| 2 | Closed | 2.4 | First setback, a laugh | Pull up, look over, then read the CLOSED sign before the reroute. |
| 3 | Boost pad | 2.2 | This game is fun | FOV punch, speed lines, BOOST! |
| 4 | LYNX dodge | 2.0 | Skill / danger | Swipe, NEAR MISS. |
| 5 | Crash + ad continue | 4.0 | Big laugh: mobile-game trope | Over the bars into tourists. Freeze on WIPED OUT. "Watch ad to continue." Parody ad. Continue from here. |
| 6 | Storm | 1.4 | Florida gag | One flash, one line. |
| 7 | Ramp over SunRail | 2.6 | Hype peak | Slow motion over the tracks, train right behind. |
| 8 | Real vs game | 3.2 | "Wait, that's a real place" | Hold long enough to compare. |
| 9 | Citi Tower pull-up | 3.6 | Arrival: we made it | Sidewalk, entrance glazing, CITITOWER sign head-on. **Hold on the sign.** |
| 10 | Elevator | 3.0 | Deflation | Press the button. Slow push on OUT OF ORDER, the music drops to silence. Dead-center deadpan stare. "…20 floors." **Let it sit.** |
| 11 | Stairs | 3.6 | Suffering, a laugh | Charge up, then **collapse at 14**, hands on knees, while Kyle texts "hello??" / "u coming or what". Last flight, 20 pops green. |
| 12 | Kyle | 3.4 | The villain, at last | Door opens, and 6'4" Kyle S **stares down in silence**. Sip. "kinda melted ngl." Shrug. Door slams. |
| 13 | Tip | 3.2 | The punchline | "Kyle S changed your tip", $50 → $0. Sad trombone. **Hold on the facepalm.** "every. single. time." |
| 14 | End | 3.0 | Share it | TIP BAIT over Citi Tower at dusk. "tag your Kyle." The new order from Kyle S loops it. |

**Sound follows the shape:**
- Phonk through the gauntlet, cutting out on the crash freeze (ad jingle) and going half-time in the slow motion.
- **Silence** from the OUT OF ORDER sign until the climb starts.
- Half-time while he's wheezing at 14.
- Tense drone under Kyle, then the beat drops out entirely for the trombone.

**Review method:** watch every shot as a 10 fps strip, and the whole cut at speed. For each beat ask:
- Did its job land?
- Was there time to read and react?
- Is anything hidden, blocked or glitching?
