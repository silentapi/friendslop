# BIG CRUMB (v2: big world)

> **You and up to 3 friends are ants. The snacks are in the house. The house is very, very far away.**

A third-person co-op game about a long trip through a giant world, hauling food home without dropping it.

It borrows from two games:
- **PEAK**: a journey where every fall costs you, and the stories come from the setbacks.
- **Big Walk**: a world you can't see all of, so talking is how you find each other.

---

## The loop

1. Leave the anthill in the corner of the backyard.
2. **Travel.** The good food is far: the patio, then the kitchen. A round trip to the kitchen takes 8–12 minutes.
3. Find a snack and **carry it home together**. Big snacks need more ants, and more ants means more ways for things to go wrong.
4. Get it to the nest and the Queen eats it. Her belly grows; you can see it.
5. Fill her before sunset. Each day the world gets a little more dangerous.

---

## Camera & controls

- **Third-person over-the-shoulder,** low to the ground. The world towers over you. You often can't see your friends, the nest, or where you're going.
- **Move, jump, climb.** Ants walk up walls, along table legs, and upside-down under ledges, which is the core traversal.
- **Hold Grab** to pick up or carry.
- **Ping** puts a scent puff in the air that friends see through walls.
- **Scent trail.** Every ant leaves a faint glowing trail for about 2 minutes. Follow a friend's trail to find them, or follow your own trail home. It's the only navigation aid: no minimap, no quest markers.

## The world (one connected map, about 20 minutes to cross on foot)

| Zone | Travel | Highlights | Dangers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Lawn** | The nest is here | Seeds, berries, dropped crumbs | Tall grass (lose sight of friends), the sprinkler, the lawn mower (a once-a-day event) |
| **The Garden Bed** | 2 min | Strawberries, a rotting apple (bonus) | Mud, the garden hose bridge, a spider web |
| **The Patio** | 4 min | Grill drippings, a dropped hot dog, chips | Hot grill grate, gaps between stones, wind |
| **The Back Door** | 5 min | The crack under the door (single file) | It opens |
| **The Kitchen** | 6–7 min | Cereal, sugar, cake, a whole donut | Counter edge (huge fall), the sink, **the Mom** |

The **path home always matters**. You choose: the short route along the counter edge (risky), or the long route down the table leg (safe, slow, and you have to climb carrying food).

---

## Setbacks (PEAK rule: every mistake costs time, nothing is ever hopeless)

- **Falling.** Ants survive any fall, bouncing and tumbling, but **the food doesn't wait for you**. It bounces, rolls, and lands somewhere else, maybe somewhere worse (the sink, under the fridge).
- **Grip.** Climbing while carrying drains a grip meter. Solo, a crumb is fine. Solo, a chip will peel you off the wall. More friends on the snack means more grip. Losing grip means a fall, which means the food is gone.
- **Narrow paths.** Twigs, the hose, the counter lip. The food is wider than the path, so someone has to walk on the edge.
- **Water.** Sprinkler drops knock you flat. The sink is a lake. Food that falls in floats away.
- **Wind** on the patio turns flat food (chips) into a sail.
- **Other ants and bugs.** A spider web catches stragglers, so friends cut you free.
- **Getting squished** (see below). You pop back at the nest after a short timer, unless a friend peels you up first.

## The Giants

Every so often a **giant human** walks into your zone. It's a big, rare, memorable event, like the PEAK storms.

**The Mom** is the homeowner archetype:
- She's in a cardigan, sweatpants and slippers, holding a coffee mug.
- She's in a hurry, and she is **terrified of bugs**.

**Warning signs** come first:
- the floor rumbles with footsteps;
- a huge shadow falls across you;
- her humming gets closer.

**Freeze or hide.** She notices **movement**. Stand still (with your food) and you're just a speck. Run and she sees you. It plays as a giant game of red-light-green-light, with friends whispering "don't. move."

**If she spots you:**
1. She SCREAMS, which shakes the camera and makes everyone drop what they're holding.
2. Then she panics: a slipper stomp, a rolled-up magazine swat, or a hiss of bug spray (a cloud you must escape).
3. After a few seconds she flees ("NOPE NOPE NOPE") and you're left scrambling to recollect the food.

Other giants show up in later zones: **Dad** with the lawn mower (backyard, loud and predictable) and **the Kid** (picks up an ant to look at it and puts it somewhere far away).

The giants are never *killers*. They're **chaos events** that scatter the team and the snack, because that's where the funny clips come from.

---

## Friends

- Proximity voice, shaped by the world: muffled through walls, echoing in pipes, a whisper when the Mom is near.
- **Carry with friends:** the snack moves in the average direction everyone walks, so you have to agree.
- **Ant chains:** grab a friend's leg to make a bridge or a rope down a ledge.
- **End-of-day awards:**
  - *Carried the Team*
  - *Fell the Furthest*
  - *Screamed At* (got spotted by the Mom)
  - *Lost the Hot Dog*

---

## Polish rounds (v2 changes)

1. **"Is the world big enough to be a journey?"** v1 was one lawn. v2 is a 20-minute-wide connected map with a clear destination you can see from the start: the house, with its lit kitchen window. The trip *is* the game; the snack is the excuse.
2. **"Are setbacks PEAK-like: costly, recoverable, funny?"** Falls cost time and position, not progress. The food is always recoverable. Every hazard has a visible warning.
3. **"Is the giant fair?"** Rumble, shadow and hum come ~3 s before she arrives, and freezing always works. Getting spotted is your fault, which is why it's funny.
4. **"Does it stay simple?"** The controls are still move, jump, grab and ping. The new systems (grip, scent trail, freeze) are all *physical*: no menus, no numbers on screen except the grip ring while you climb.
