# OVER BUDGET: the simple version (v0.3)

> 🔨 fix stuff, get paid. break your friends' stuff, they don't. just don't break so much that grandma fires everyone.

This replaces the complicated parts of v0.1 and v0.2. **If a rule doesn't fit on this page, it's not in the game.**

---

## The whole game on one card

| | Rule |
|---|---|
| 🎯 **Goal** | Get the house to **100% fixed** before Grandma arrives (about 8 minutes). |
| 💵 **Scoring** | Every job you finish puts **cash in your pocket** (+$50, +$100…). Most cash at the end wins. |
| 😈 **Sabotage** | Walk up to any job someone *else* finished and **hold F to wreck it**. They lose that cash, and the job goes back on the board for anyone to re-fix (and re-earn). |
| 👵 **The catch** | When Grandma arrives, if the house isn't 100%, **nobody gets paid** for that house. |

That's the game. The fun is in the tension between the last two rows: *wreck Kev's toilet so you pull ahead, but the clock is ticking and if it's still broken when Grandma shows up, you're all broke.*

---

## How it plays

**1. Arrive.** The van pulls up and everyone piles out. The house has about 12 broken things, each with a glowing **!** marker you can see through walls:
- a leaky toilet
- a hole in the wall
- a busted light
- a wobbly stair
- a bathtub that's in the yard for some reason

**2. Race for jobs.** Any job, anyone, first come first served. Each job is a tiny minigame of about 5–10 seconds: hold E, tap E, or carry the thing over. Finish it and a **"+$100 KEV"** pops up in your colour over the job. It's like grabbing coins in Mario Party: you're competing for the same jobs.

**3. Wreck stuff.**
- Hold F on someone else's finished job for 3 seconds.
- **It breaks in a big, dumb, visible way:** the toilet sprays water, the wall collapses, sparks fly. Their money flies off as coins.
- **Anyone can re-fix it for the cash,** including you. That's the steal.
- **It isn't sneaky.** It's loud and obvious, Mario Kart-shell energy. Your friends will see it and yell at you. That's the point.

**4. Get them back.** You don't have to spy or catch anyone. The defence is the same as the attack: **slap them** (shove button), so they ragdoll and drop what they're holding, or just re-fix it before anyone else does.

**5. Grandma arrives.** The bar at the top shows **HOUSE 87%**. At 0:00 she walks in.
- **100%:** everyone banks their cash, plus a bonus for whoever did the most jobs.
- **Not 100%:** a funny fail cutscene plays (she flushes, the toilet explodes), and **nobody banks this house's cash.**

**6. Next house.** 3 houses per game. The last house is a mansion. Most cash wins, and last place has to wear the **dunce hard hat** next game.

## Why this is simpler

| Before | Now |
|--------|-----|
| Work orders, a Foreman, assignments | Jobs are just markers on the map. Grab any. |
| Proper vs. hack per task, hidden flaws | Jobs are done or not done. |
| Signatures, forgery, evidence photos, blackmail, timesheets, blame votes | Sabotage is visible. Revenge is a slap. |
| Client suspicion meters, cover-up verbs, walkthrough routes | Grandma checks one number: is the house 100%? |
| Side hustles, skimming, tips, docking, awards | One number: your cash. |

**What stays:**
- the silly bean workers
- a real 3D house you run around
- yelling at friends
- Grandma
- the "nobody gets paid" disaster

## Optional spice (only if playtests feel flat)

Add **one** of these at a time, never all of them:
- **Golden job:** one random job is worth triple. Everyone stampedes toward it.
- **Power tools:** pick up a nail gun or leaf blower that makes your jobs faster *or* knocks people over.
- **Grandma's early:** sometimes the timer suddenly drops by 60 seconds.
- **Revenge pays:** re-fixing a job someone wrecked pays +50% extra.
